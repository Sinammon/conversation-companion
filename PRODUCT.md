# Conversation Companion

## Platform

Adaptive native app for iOS and Android, built with Expo and TypeScript.

## Students and their work

A student opens the app as a lecture or discussion begins, checks that everyone agrees to transcription, then follows readable live captions. After class they can revisit the transcript, review structured notes, and ask questions against the same conversation. Students who rely on captions must be able to tell when the microphone stops.

## Interaction design

Use a quiet notebook-like library, a clear recording action, and a distraction-free caption reader. Use a warm paper background, dark ink, a restrained green accent, generous tap targets, system text scaling and explicit listening states. Start with an empty local notebook. The primary language for this first release is US English.
